About SmartFiles

SmartFiles helps you securely store, organize, and find your files on your computer and phone.

It runs locally on your device and is designed to make file management simple and reliable — with no cloud dependency.

Protect your files and reduce the risk of data loss

Quickly find any file without manual searching

Keep your files organized automatically

Manage everything locally for better privacy and control

SmartFiles is built for everyday users who want a simple way to keep their files safe, organized, and easy to access.
